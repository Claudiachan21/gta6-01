Solmar Bay — an original 3D open-world crime game in the browser: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a 5-star wanted level with police chases and roadblocks, a 6-mission story, combat, a day/night cycle, a chop shop, 9 named districts, landmarks, a HUD with minimap, and a save. Static files, no network requests, keyboard and mouse.

## What's in the city

- **6 missions**: deliveries, a car theft, a harbor shootout, a timed sunset run, a 90-second last stand at 5 stars, and a night-time heist.
- **Day/night cycle**: a 6-minute day, golden hour, lit windows after dark, a rotating lighthouse beam.
- **Sunset Auto garage**: sell stolen cars, bribe the heat down, repaint your ride.
- **9 named districts** with toasts, a stadium, a harbor crane, a Ferris wheel, a lighthouse, a casino sign, a market and a construction site.
- **12 cash stashes** — some buried on the beach, some only safe to take when you're clean.
- **Vehicles**: sedans, sport, muscle, pickup, van, taxi, limo, truck and police — each with its own handling.
- **Radio**: three synth stations (T), photo mode (F), pause-screen stats.

## Controls

WASD move / drive · Shift run · drag mouse look · Click shoot · R reload · E enter / steal car · Space handbrake · G garage (sell / bribe) · B repaint · T radio · F photo mode · M mute · Esc pause

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
